##   **![[KingOlay.png]]Name**: King Olay
## Real name: Patrick Highfield
## Age: 78 (29 at time of crisis)
- Birthday: 19/4/90

## Inspiration:
Olay (formerly Oil of Olay) is a globally recognised American skincare brand offering a comprehensive range of face and body products like moisturisers, **cleansers**, serums, and anti-aging treatments.
Ngl, also taking a lot of inspiration from Gwyneth Paltrow and her Goop thing.
I find the "Body Vibes", wearable stickers that were claimed to "rebalance the energy frequency in our bodies" and which Goop falsely claimed were made of a NASA-developed material, the funniest - don't think it as fully in-line with Patrick's character but we'll see.
POOH is a parody of GOOP (Gwyneth said that G.P came from her initial and that all of her successful businesses have two Os; for PH, all of his successful businesses have two vowels)
## Backstory
Billionaire owner of the Olay Health conglomerate, with its constituent companies being the real-life Olay, Mr Clean brands and PEAH. He spends his whole life obsessing over his own health and avoiding death, only interrupting this negative cycle by writing speeches detailing his progress for a body double to deliver.
**Why didn't Patrick take the cream in these 10 years, if it was proven to be safe and aligned perfectly with his goals?**
In his new persona as King Olay, Olay is now the proud saviour of humanity, the one who has, at least in a region of the world, managed to rid the world of dirt and sickness.
## Description: 
Patrick, during the timeline of Tom's story, will never reveal his face. If he is to die, it should be via a small crack in his armour.
- White
- Tall - 178cm
- Lean - makes much effort to work out until he begins to sweat
#### What we see:
- Submarine pane in yellow body suit - allows him to breathe whilst interacting as little as possible with his external environment
	- Extra thick to ensure that breaches into the suit are next to impossible
- Regal outfit
	- Must maintain an aura of composure in order to coerce the masses into following his orders, that his plan can go without fail - it's a persona as he isn't this bold normally
	- Helps that him being royalty means that he can employ others to keep them filthy commoners away from him
## Personality: 
- Bashful and ashamed
	- Unable to address and admit to his mistakes. Becomes increasingly avoidant and panicked when his errors are addressed
- Self-diminishing
	- Constantly relies on his accolades and the affirmation of others in order to not think too little of himself. Slowly begins to love the king persona as it feeds into both of these guttural desires.
- Caring
	- Truly wants the best of people - he wants them to achieve their goals in life, such as that perfect physique or an unparalleled confidence
	- Cannot understand those who are content with imperfection - perplexed by them but determined to share their mind (this is his only real source of confidence)
## Motivation: 
-  Wants to create a perfect world where no sickness, death and suffering exists and everyone can be their ideal selves.
	- Maybe Patrick thought he was doing the world a favour by giving them skincare products to make them younger, only for them to be found to have the horrible skin effect of decaying the flesh from within and turning them into horrible monsters?
## Hobbies:
- Would like to convince the world of the validity of his methods - they all think of him as yet another dumb billionaire with more money than sense.
- Studies medicine thoroughly to best understand the human body and its limits
	- You have to know everything in order to best see how to improve it
## Likes and dislikes:
### Likes:
-  Being clean
- Having his efforts respected - makes him feel good about himself
- Learning about the human body
	- Through his pedantic study of medicine to better understand how to extend his life, he slowly came to appreciate its intricacies and how delicately it needs to be treated
### Dislikes:
- Germaphobe - genuinely hates all forms of filth with a passion
	- Even sweat he despises as he knows it is a form of excrement - having his whole body coated in the stuff is enough to trigger a meltdown.
- Aging - he ultimately fears weakness in all forms, so he wants to create a perfect world where no sickness, death and suffering exists.
