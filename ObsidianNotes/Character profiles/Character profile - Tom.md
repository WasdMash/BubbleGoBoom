## ![[Head.png|right|100]]  **Name**: Thomas Richter
## Age: 11
- Birthday: 9/10/57

## Inspiration:
Tom is a caricature of how kids are perceived by older generations - lazy and unwilling to work except for their own goals. On the contrary, Tom's aspirations are greater than most and he is willing to sacrifice everything but time in order to achieve these goals, even his friends.
## Description: 
Tom is a brunette, eyeless little boy with a simple clothing style - green shirt and blue shorts. Around 150cm
## Personality: 
- Stubborn
	- doesn't listen to authority figures (rebellious) because he always wants to do things his way. 
	- Finds obedience to be boring and will  Impulsive and goes for the first thing he sees.
- Self-centred
	- Dresses for comfort because he doesn't care about the opinions of interests of others, unless if they serve his.
## Motivation: 
- Always chooses the most reckless action possible in order to have more fun his way.
- Ultimately wants to live a cool life that many will tell tales about - wants to be like Master Chief, one whose influence crosses through genres
	- Anything which gets in the way of this is viewed as filler and must be eliminated
## Hobbies:
- Playing games - favourite is Halo because he can do so many things (e.g drive cars, drag stuff around mid-air) whilst still brutally killing aliens without remorse
- Re-enacting the stories of his favourite hero, Zorro, recorded on a cassette tape he got from his grandparents (because Kaiketsu Zoro is like from the 90s)
- Imagining the scenarios that local legend Guillaume Tell could get up to in the modern day
	- Must be read a bedtime story of Guillaume by his parents every night before he goes to sleep

## Likes and dislikes:
### Likes:
- Roaming unrestricted
- Daydreaming
	- Imagining wild stories, especially the ones where he is the hero

### Dislikes:
- Cleaning his room - it's such a boring chore
- Being told what to do
- Planning
	- It's boring and wastes important time in developing the plot (ironic and he will need to be taught how planning makes action better)
- Hates being called short
	- Sees himself as a grand hero and being called short sounds patronising. He must humble them and put them in their place
