<h1>Tom's lore - start of game story</h1>
- We start as a regular kid in the Clean Kingdom called Tom. We find him just lounging around in his especially dirty bedroom, just daydreaming about all of the heroes that he could be.
	- Tom snook some of his food into his shirt (to use as a quicksand trap for his action figure) as his parents nodded sleepily at the dinner table
- His parents panic and frantically rush to clean the room with him (as they repeatedly have to each week)
	- if they are found out, then King Olay will have them arrested and banished to the horrible Dirt Kingdom, where filth rules over all (any source of dirt must be banished).
- Tom, unwillingly, pretends to help his parents clean the room as they finish just in time before the weekly house inspection begins.
	- Oh no, Tom's parents aren't able to get their dirty boy out of the house in time. They're quite stressed out now
- The inspection goes quite well, with no even a speck of dirt identified anywhere.
	- This is until Tom's room is inspected - the inspector ruffles through Tom's toys, ruining the world he had created. Tom gets in a rage and tries to beat up the inspector. His parents restrain him as the angry inspector threatens to use force. Tom, then, wriggles an arm free to grab some of the hidden slop in his pocket and throws it in the face of the inspector.
	- This slop is now considered a contraband slime and thus, the Richter house now fails the inspection
- They are immediately arrested and thus, banished to the Dirt Kingdom where the rest of the filth belongs
	- King Olay forbids the banished to be escorted at the same time, for what if they band together to create more filth. Can't be having that.
	- As such, the father is the first to be banished.
		- The mother is distraught in tears, yelling at Tom whom she now holds responsible for the loss of their father. Tom, being the ungrateful little twit that he is, shows no remorse and accepts no responsibility for this - he's only mad that he couldn't hit the inspector hard enough.
	- The next day, the mother is banished.
		- Now, Tom realises that with nobody in the house to whine at him, he can do what he wants. He also still wants to beat up that inspector for ruining his hard work. So he waits for that same inspector to come and get his mother, planning to ambush him.
		- He successfully manages to steal his bubble gun without noticing and hides it behind his back as the inspector pins him down with a man-catcher and uses a laser dazzler to flashbang him.
		- Tom is left dazed on the floor, as he watches his mother gets pushed into the cop car for her banishment.
	- When Tom wakes up, he knows that he has to run, otherwise his gun will be confiscated
		- He then runs as far as he can but eventually collapses on the side of the road
		- Fade to black
		- When he awakens, he finds himself in an abandoned dungeon in the Dirt Kingdom
- SHOCK FACTOR: King Olay has found out that Tom managed to successfully steal a gun from the inspector. He knows of Tom's fight against the inspector and how it required two adults to pin him down. He even knows of Tom's desire to be the hero in his own story. Let's kill two birds with one stone!
	- He gets the crazy idea of giving Tom a chance to redeem himself - Tom can keep the gun, as long as he proves himself worthy as humanity's greatest warrior, killing all of the filthy monsters in the Dirt Kingdom.
	- Apparently, most of these dirty monsters like to hide out in abandoned dungeons, where they can congregate and mass-produce dirt. Tom must use his sneakiness to infiltrate these dungeons and eradicate them all.
	- If he can do so, Tom will become the first person to return from banishment and be honoured as King Olay's top bodyguard, Defender of the Clean Kingdom.
	- Tom, as a naïve little kid who doesn't question the logistics behind any of this, agrees only for his own glory, hoping that by him killing those bad guys, he can squeeze information out of King Olay in order to find that pesky inspector
- **As such, this is where we enter into the main gameplay loop of the game - just kill the bad guys in the dungeon until they are all dead - once then are, fade to black and enter the next one**

## The story unfolds - what happens next
- Olay points Tom to each of the big dungeons, telling Tom that he can keep whatever loot he finds
	- These are the weapons left behind by all the other unsuccessful individuals that King Olay sent out to complete Tom's mission
	- Wow, this is what the banished have been sent out to do - is this the real reason why people are banished one at a time, to hide this information?
- Tom goes through all of the kingdoms, successfully killing all of the enemies and the monster bosses co-ordinating the masses
- After the completion of the second realm, Tom finally finds the inspector
	- King Olay doesn't risk contamination and thus expels all inspectors and guards involved in the banishment of criminals.
	- This inspector has rounded his own faction of survivors in this second realm.
	- Tom, finally seeing the chance to get revenge, goes for the attack, only to find that the inspector still remains unarmed
		- Would the hero really kill an unarmed man? How then should he forgive the man who banished him to this wasteland to die?
- Tom continues with his heroic mission and clears the third and final realm of the Dirt Kingdom
- As such, Tom tries to contact King Olay and tell him that the job is done and he is ready to be proclaimed as hero
	- However, King Olay just gives him a Professor Oak congratulations and hangs up
	- Tom rightly feels betrayed and wants answers, so he crawls back to the walls of the Clean Kingdom
- The bubble is only one-way - there was never a way back into the Kingdom, so King Olay lied! Tom, in line with the story opening, hides in the bushes outside of the gate, waiting for yet another banishment to occur.
	- He eventually finds his chance and sneaks in behind the cop car before the wall closes .
	- Nice. Now, it's time to get revenge on King Olay.
- King Olay learns of this breach of the wall and immediately sends out a bounty for Tom
	- Most citizens recognise Tom's face from the banishment a couple of years ago (assumes that it took a couple of years for Tom to accomplish his mission) but don't know why he was banished or that it was even possible to come back.
	- This, rightly causes curiosity and some of the dissenters hide Tom away as they question him
	- Tom tells them what Olay had put him through, how he was ripped away from his parents etc
- They sympathise and concoct a plan to smuggle him before the ever reclusive King Olay.
- Tom battles through the forces of Olay's lackeys in his place and eventually comes face to face with Olay
- Olay, afraid of the monster that he created (both the monsters outside and the enraged Tom that stands before him), attempts to jump from the roof, knowing that his thick suit will protect him from the fall.
	- He unfortunately looks down to see a riot formed outside his window - Tom has incapacitated his guards during the break-in
- Olay, in fight-or-flight mode, engages in battle with Tom, trying to kill him
- Tom kills King Olay during this battle and stands over Olay in his final moments
	- Olay, forced by death to confront the consequences of his actions, finally admits to Tom the truth of what he has done, how his blind pursuit to see humanity flourish led him to rush out the de-aging product, how each monster out there was a human, how he created his persona to avoid blame and staged the monster ambushes to appease his germaphobia.
	- His final words, he tells Tom, "You were a kid who knew no better, I knew." He then dies
- Tom is now forced to confront what he has done
	- He's been mercilessly killing thousands of enemies and realises that this isn't what heroes do- **should we keep count of how many monsters he has killed?**
	- In the shattering of his hero dreams, he's now forced to confront the guilt of what he's done, the blood which Olay has now forced on his hands.
- In shame, Tom walks out of the Clean Kingdom for good, never to return again
