Someone looked at Patrick Highfield's research and perfects the method of extending people's lives forever.

## Setting
- This is the supposed utopia that everyone expects of humanity. What are the implications of it?
	- Everyone is now able to do whatever they want, unrestrained by their bodily weakness. What if people want to commit crime?
		- We should ban drugs because their usage and resulting negligence would result in disposed needles and the such.
		- Recreational procreation, strip clubs and bars are banned - all of these places result in spilt liquids and mess everywhere. How would people initially respond to this, considering the Prohibition and how that went?
			- Government goes full Orwellian mode and casts all of the drunkards out - initial anger but then replaced by an intense fear until eventually, later generations forget and just comply
		- The main kind of crime now is white-collar: embezzlement
	- Nobody can get sick anymore, woohoo? What happens to the doctors? What do they do?
