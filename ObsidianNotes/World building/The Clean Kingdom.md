## Inspiration
- Lowkey kind of Orwellian (1984 and Big Brother type timing)
	- Have to ensure that everyone complies, otherwise we'll just have more dirt and our hard work is undone
	- However, everyone will comply because who doesn't want a healthy, eternal life to be their best selves?
- The level of control required to enforce such a world is inspired by Chinese and North Korean authorities (easily understood by the Western audience as controlling and dictatory)
- The location is inspired by Zurich, Switzerland due to its strong environmental rules and strict waste management systems.
	- Patrick would love it here. It's also probably the easiest place for him to implement his regime with little resistance
	- However, due to Switzerland being a very strong consumer of alcohol (11.5 litres per person in comparison to 5 litres), dismantling the alcohol culture could prove very troublesome.

## History [Huge spoiler warning]
- We start in 2009 in this world
- Billionaire Patrick Highfield makes a lot of money selling cleaning and skincare products, motivated to cleanse the world and push the human race to its limits
- He invents a cream medically proven to reverse the effects of aging by 10 years
	- This thus in theory creates a profit loop where people are dependent on his product
	- In theory, it should last for 10 years, so in the initial testing phase of the product, no harmful side effects were found
		- Lo and behold, the side effects only occur on the second usage as it triggers a severe chemical reaction to applied flesh
- This cream decays the flesh of those who use it, turning them into horrible, misshapen monsters.
- Those who remained alive were the following, who didn't use the cream:
	- Conspiracy theorists
	- The physically active - their activity would already keep their bodies young
	- Children and young adults - they didn't need to be younger - they are already young
- Some are killed by these zombies - the rest congregate in factions around the world
- Those in Europe flocked to a safe zone marked out and defended by a mysterious individual known as Olay
	- With his many products and defensive techniques, he successfully rallies the remaining survivors together and vanquishes all of the filthy creatures in Zurich.
	- He teaches the people how to create cleaning supply weapons to defend themselves, thus enabling him to expand his reach to encompass the whole nation
- The people are indebted to his genius and declare him the head of the army fighting to protect against the filthy monsters
	- In the first couple of years (2020 to 2022), Olay is often invited to people's feasts or pub crawls for celebration - Olay, being Patrick, hates the idea of filth still being pervaded, so initially he reluctantly comes along, always refusing to take the suit off.
	- Then, he has an idea one day
		- He sees a group of monsters rummaging through rubbish, completely ignoring the spectators nearby who stand amazed. Olay proudly declares his discovery, "these horrid creatures must be attracted to filth, so we must get rid of all filth."
		- Initially, everyone thinks of this as a harmless conjecture but they later see that in the areas that Olay cleaned up the most, there were the fewest monsters, and so eventually, everyone adopts this practice.
	- Olay wants to get rid of all monsters and still despises the filth of bars, so he pretends to go to the toilet and sneaks a monster into the pub before hiding in the toilet. The monster rampages through the bar, where most are unarmed, causing the loss of life of a few before Olay proudly barges out of the toilet and kills the monsters using the toilet soap dispensers.
		- He again presents the conjecture of filth attracting monsters, suggesting that the spilt alcohol all over the bar attracted the monster inside. Some disagree but during the vehemently emotional state that the witnesses are in, they reluctantly agree to cut down on the alcohol consumption
		- Some smuggle it into their homes for private parties but get outed by Olay's most loyal men. Monsters are once again drawn to some of these homes in attack (as we'll find out later, were once again planted) and as a result, alcohol is entirely banned in the nation.
- Once again, the people are indebted to Olay's wit and resourcefulness, establishing him now as the ruler of the whole nation
	- Over time, Olay pushes for more restrictions, banning strip clubs and recreational drugs, using his lackeys to banish any of the disobedient to the unchartered territories beyond, which due to the fear mongered before, he can label simply as "the Dirt Kingdom".
	- As such, those who were employed in such sectors were either forced to get different jobs in this economy, or if they staunchly opposed to the removal of their sector, were banished to the Dirt Kingdom as an example to the others.
		- Dirt Kingdom might have a couple of shady bars, drug dens and strip clubs for the player to loot through as a result of this.
		- Could be the reason for dissenting factions who hate the Clean Kingdom to be formed in the Dirt Kingdom
			- Living on veg in the previously uninhabited parts of the world, all Dr Stone-like
- Olay slowly becomes drunk on the power and morphs from this socially reclusive hero and dispenser of wisdom to a more Kim-like 'deity among men' archetype, demanding full obedience to him
	- This is where we truly start to look like an Orwellian society - should probably read the book at this point
	- He has fully now retracted to the shadows to live on his own
